Methods

Six computational workflows

Fields describe the science; workflows describe the work. One field can use several methods.

W1Molecular electronic structure

Predict a molecule's energy, geometry or electronic transitions and compare with a measured observable.

Related fields: C1 · Catalysis C3 · Mechanism & function C4 · Structure & dynamics C7 · Theory & computation

W2First principles for periodic systems

Calculate a property of a surface or crystal and check it against published values.

Related fields: C1 · Catalysis M2 · Ceramics M3 · Materials theory M5 · Electronic & photonic M8 · Solid-state chemistry

W3Molecular simulation

Sample the configurations of a molecular system to estimate quantities such as binding or gas uptake.

Related fields: C8 · Life processes C9 · Supramolecular & nano M7 · Polymers M8 · Solid-state chemistry

W4Kinetics and multiscale modeling

Connect microscopic events to observable rates, morphologies or phase behavior.

Related fields: C1 · Catalysis C4 · Structure & dynamics C6 · Systems & solutions M6 · Metals & alloys

W5Data-driven modeling

Learn properties or screen candidates, then measure performance on held-out data.

Related fields: C5 · Synthesis C7 · Theory & computation M5 · Electronic & photonic M8 · Solid-state chemistry

W6Experimental data interpretation

Infer structure from spectra or diffraction, then compare with a known reference.

Related fields: C2 · Measurement & imaging M4 · Condensed matter
